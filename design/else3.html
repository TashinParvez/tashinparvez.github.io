01 — Origin

How I got here

Dhaka · 2026

It started with breaking things, not building them.

I was a CS student at UIU, deep in Codeforces problems at 2 AM. Somewhere around my 800th problem, a quiet realization: I was more excited about why something should exist than how the algorithm worked.

"The moment I stopped asking 'how does this compile' and started asking 'who actually needs this' — that's when product thinking clicked."

Teaching made me a PM before I knew it.

As a TA across four courses, I spent semesters watching students struggle — not with code, but with understanding what the problem actually was. That instinct — diagnosing real friction, not assumed friction — is exactly what I do now as a Product Analyst at Ontik Technology.

What I'm building toward.

I want to work on B2B SaaS products where the users are professionals, the workflows are messy, and good product decisions directly move business metrics.

02 — Product Beliefs

Things I hold true

01

Start with the problem, not the feature.

Most bad products skip the "why." I push to understand real friction before proposing anything.

02

Features are hypotheses, not commitments.

Ship small, learn fast. Ego has no place in a product roadmap.

03

Enterprise users deserve consumer-grade UX.

"It's B2B" is not an excuse for bad design. Professionals notice when a product respects their time.

04

Data tells you what. Conversations tell you why.

Analytics without user context is guessing with charts. I use both.
